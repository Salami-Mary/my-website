Introduction

Hi 👋 I'm Mary Salami, a PhD candidate in Geographic Information Science at the University of California, Santa Barbara. My research sits at the intersection of generative AI and computational sustainability — applying machine learning and geospatial analysis to climate mitigation and renewable energy.

Recent work includes a generative-AI tool for tracking local opposition to energy siting across U.S. counties, and spatiotemporal models of human and environmental systems. Before UCSB, I was a research scientist at Data Science Nigeria, building computer-vision and NLP datasets centered on African representation. I care about work that is technically rigorous and grounded in the places and communities it describes.
